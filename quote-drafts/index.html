How it works

  1. 1

    It reads the request

    Lane, equipment, weight, pickup date — even when the whole request is only in the subject line, spread across a few replies in the same thread, or has typos in city names. Several lanes in one email are handled separately.

  2. 2

    It checks your own rate history

    A Google Sheet with your past loads. A lane you've run before gets the median of your last five loads on it. Only the same states? You get a range to pick from. No history at all? The draft says so instead of guessing a number. Details missing from the request? The draft asks the shipper for them.

  3. 3

    It leaves a draft, not a sent email

    The draft goes into the same Gmail thread, labelled Quote ready. Invoices and anything that isn't a rate request get labelled Not a quote and no draft is written. You open it, check it, press Send.

What it does not do

  • It never sends an email by itself. A person always opens the draft, checks it, and presses Send.
  • It does not set your prices. Rates come only from your own history sheet; you decide what actually goes out.
  • No history on a lane means a plain placeholder in the draft, never a made-up number.
  • Cities are matched by name. "Dallas" and "Fort Worth" are treated as different lanes.
  • Gmail and Google Workspace only, for now — no Outlook.
  • The email text is read by an AI model through my own proxy to write the draft; it is not used to train models.

Installation by email, no calls

Everything happens over written email. No video calls, no screen shares, nothing to install on your computer.

  1. You get a link to a Google Sheet with your settings already filled in. You click Make a copy, then give it access to your Gmail on the Google screens that follow. About five minutes.
  2. You reply with the sheet shared back to me. I load your rate history and adjust the wording to match how you write.
  3. We test it on your real requests by email until the drafts are right, then it runs on its own.

FAQ

Is my email data safe?

The text of a rate request is sent to an AI model (Anthropic's API, through a proxy I run) so it can write the draft. The model sees only the last few messages of that thread — no attachments, no other emails, no sheet data. That text is not stored afterwards and is not used to train any model. Your rate history stays in your own Google Sheet the whole time.

Will Google show a warning when I give it access?

On a Google Workspace address on your own domain, usually not. On a personal gmail.com address, yes: Google shows "Google hasn't verified this app", because the script runs as your own copy inside your account and you are the "developer" of that copy. You click Advanced, then Go to Quote Assistant (unsafe), then Continue, tick Select all, and Continue again. I tell you in the setup email exactly what you'll see, so it isn't a surprise.

Does it work with Outlook?

Not yet. Right now it only runs inside Gmail and Google Workspace.

How long does setup take?

About five minutes on your side to copy the sheet and give it access. After that we go back and forth by email, testing it on your real requests, until every draft looks right — that part moves at whatever pace suits you.

What if it turns out not to fit my inbox?

If we test it and it doesn't work for how your rate requests come in, whatever you paid up front is refunded.

Can an agency resell this to its broker clients?

Yes. Agencies and consultants who set up inboxes for their own broker clients can offer it white-label, by agreement — write in and we'll work out the terms.

Will it ever send an email without me?

No. It only ever leaves a draft in the thread. Sending is always a person clicking Send.
