cra-report

EU Cyber Resilience Act Article 14 reporting tool

Since 11 September 2026 a manufacturer placing a product with digital elements on the EU market has 24 hours to notify ENISA of an actively exploited vulnerability in it. Finding vulnerabilities is not the hard part - any scanner hands you a hundred. cra-report says which of them starts the statutory clock, and drafts the Article 14 notification for the ones that do.

Try it: npx cra-report

Source on GitHub Work with me

What it does

  • Reads a CycloneDX or SPDX SBOM, package-lock.json, Cargo.lock or a pinned requirements.txt.
  • Asks OSV what is known about those exact versions, across npm, PyPI, crates.io, Go, Maven, NuGet, RubyGems and Packagist.
  • Cross-references every CVE against the CISA KEV catalogue - the list of vulnerabilities observed being exploited in the wild.
  • Writes the Article 14(1) early warning with both deadlines counted from now, and marks what only a person can answer.

How it is proved

On a dependency tree containing log4j-core 2.14.1 it reports 2 findings with a deadline out of 19 advisories, both flagged as used in ransomware campaigns. On lodash 4.17.15 it reports 0 out of 6 - equally worth being able to say.
