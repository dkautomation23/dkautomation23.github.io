whatsapp-crm-connector

WhatsApp Business Cloud webhooks into a CRM

The parts of a WhatsApp integration that are not the happy path: signature verification, an instant acknowledgement with the work moved to a queue, de-duplication of repeated deliveries, conversations logged against both the contact and the company, the 24-hour window, and token refresh.

Source on GitHub Work with me

What it does

  • Verifies the Meta signature over the raw body, and rejects a forged one.
  • Answers within milliseconds and processes afterwards, because Meta retries anything slow.
  • Runs offline with one command, against recorded payloads.

How it is proved

42 tests, including a forged signature that must be refused.
