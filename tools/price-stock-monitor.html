price-stock-monitor

Price and stock changes, with the history to prove them

Operators track competitor prices and restocks by opening tabs and typing numbers into a spreadsheet, which means they learn about a change whenever they next look. Point this at a product URL with a CSS selector and it watches on a schedule, keeps every observation, and says what moved and by how much.

Source on GitHub Work with me

What it does

  • A CSS selector rather than a per-shop integration, so Shopify, WooCommerce, a marketplace and a hand-written page are all the same case.
  • Every observation is stored, so a claim about a price two weeks ago can be checked instead of remembered.
  • Change detection reports the delta and the direction, including out-of-stock and back-in-stock, which is usually the more valuable of the two.
