lead-responder

Answer the enquiry while they are still on the page

Most small businesses answer a web enquiry whenever someone next opens the inbox — after lunch, next morning, Monday. By then the person has messaged three competitors. The fix is not a bigger CRM: it is replying in under a second, and not spending that speed on the job applications and SEO pitches that arrive through the same form.

Source on GitHub Work with me

What it does

  • Scores the enquiry, writes a reply that quotes what they actually asked, and offers real slots from a calendar rather than “we will be in touch”.
  • Escalates what is worth a human and quietly files what is not — the spam arrives through the same form as the money.
  • Runs offline end to end with --demo, so the behaviour can be judged before any credential is issued.
